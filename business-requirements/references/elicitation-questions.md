# Elicitation Questions for Business Requirements

Use these targeted questions to guide stakeholders through defining the "Why" and "What" of their project.

## 1. Defining the Business Opportunity
*   What is the primary business problem we are trying to solve?
*   What is the market or internal opportunity we want to exploit?
*   What happens if we *don't* do this project?
*   What is the history or context that led to this decision?

## 2. Iterative Goal Identification (The "Five Whys" for Objectives)
Use these to drill down from vague goals to measurable objectives:

*   **From Objective to Problem:** "What is keeping us from achieving this goal at present?"
*   **From Goal to Rationale:** "Why do we care about that specific goal?"
*   **From Problem to Objective:** "How will we objectively assess whether this problem is solved?"

## 3. Setting SMART Business Objectives
*   What quantitative benefits do we expect (e.g., revenue increase, cost savings, efficiency)?
*   By how much? (Get a number: 20%, $100k, 15 minutes).
*   By when? (Get a timeframe: within 6 months, by Q4).
*   Is this objective truly relevant to our top-level strategy?

## 4. Success Metrics (KPIs)
*   How will we know we are on track *before* the project is fully complete?
*   What can we measure during testing or in the first few weeks after launch?
*   What are the critical success factors we can track immediately?

## 5. Product Vision (The Elevator Pitch)
Guide the stakeholder through this template:
*   **For:** Who is the target customer?
*   **Who:** What is their need or opportunity?
*   **The:** What is the product name?
*   **Is a:** What category of product is it?
*   **That:** What is the single most compelling benefit?
*   **Unlike:** What is the current process or main competitor?
*   **Our product:** What is our primary differentiation?

## 6. Project Priorities & Constraints
*   Of Features, Quality, Schedule, Cost, and Staff:
    *   Which one is a **Constraint** (fixed/limiting)?
    *   Which one is a **Driver** (key success factor)?
    *   Which ones are **Degrees of Freedom** (flexible/negotiable)?
