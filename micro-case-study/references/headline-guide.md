# Headline Guide

The headline is the client's pain point stated plainly. Not what you built, not the outcome, not a marketing tagline. A clear, descriptive sentence that tells the reader what the client was dealing with.

## How to Write a Headline

1. Ask: what was the client's concern before you showed up?
2. Write a clear, descriptive sentence stating that concern
3. No specific names — use roles (CEO, staff, team) instead
4. Be descriptive, not snarky or vague — the reader should understand the problem immediately
5. No metrics in headlines — those belong in the Value column

## Bad to Good Examples

| Bad (Describes Your Work) | Also Bad (Marketing Tagline) | Good (Plain Statement of Pain) |
|---|---|---|
| Inactivity Email Automation | Stop Losing Students to Inconsistent Follow-Up | Students are going inactive and nobody is following up consistently. |
| Chapter Completion Automation | Get Chapter Completion Emails Off the CEO's Plate | The CEO is personally stuck sending emails every time a student finishes a chapter. |
| Transcript Status Overhaul | Trust Your Student Records Before the State Audits Them | Student records are wrong, and wrong records could fail a state audit. |
| Service Plan Form + Auto-PDF | Give Inactive Students a Clear Path Back | When a student goes inactive, there's no structured way to bring them back. |
| Task Assignment Interface | See Who on Your Team Is Doing What | There's no way to see which team members are assigned to which students or tasks. |
| Agreement Form + Ad Order Creation | Stop Signed Deals from Sitting in a Pile | Signed deals sit in a pile before anyone starts production. |
| QuickBooks Invoice Integration | Stop Jumping Between Systems to Send an Invoice | Assembling and sending invoices takes too long and involves too many systems. |
| Data Migration & Deduplication | Answer Basic Questions About Your Own Sales History | They can't answer basic questions about their own sales history. |
| Contact Consolidation | Find the Right Contact Without Guessing | Nobody knows where to find the right contact for a given ad. |
| Income Account Routing | See Revenue by Publication | Leadership can't see how much revenue each publication is generating. |
| DocuSign Replacement | Trust Your Student Records | All student data is trapped in PDFs that can't be searched or linked to student records. |
| PDF Parser Tool | Stop Re-Typing Every Estimate | Each estimate requires hours of manual re-entry from PDF to project management system. |
| Schedule Scraper | See Future Crew Capacity | Future crew scheduling data exists but can't be exported or reported on. |

## Key Principles

- The headline is the client's pain stated plainly, not the service and not a tagline
- Ask "what was the client worried about?" before writing the headline
- Be descriptive and specific, not clever or snarky
- Use the client's language when possible; if they said "I can't see who's doing what," that's the headline
- No specific names — use roles (CEO, staff, leadership, team)
- Metrics belong in the Value column, not the headline
