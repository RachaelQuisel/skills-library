# Time to Money Cheat Sheet

Use when an MCS involves time or cost recovery but no specific dollar figure is cited.

| Hrs/Wk | Hrs/Yr | @$25/hr | @$35/hr | @$50/hr |
|--------|--------|---------|---------|---------|
| 5      | 260    | $6,500  | $9,100  | $13,000 |
| 10     | 520    | $13,000 | $18,200 | $26,000 |
| 15     | 780    | $19,500 | $27,300 | $39,000 |
| 20     | 1,040  | $26,000 | $36,400 | $52,000 |
| 25     | 1,300  | $32,500 | $45,500 | $65,000 |
| 30     | 1,560  | $39,000 | $54,600 | $78,000 |
| 40     | 2,080  | $52,000 | $72,800 | $104,000 |

**Formula:** Hours/Week x 52 = Hours/Year x Hourly Rate = Annual Value

When a transcript mentions time savings but no dollar amount:
1. Identify hours saved per week from the transcript
2. Pick the closest row in the table
3. Choose an hourly rate based on the role (admin ~$25/hr, coordinator ~$35/hr, manager ~$50/hr)
4. Report the annual value and mark Data Confidence as **Estimated**
