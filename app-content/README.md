# app-content

Automate the weekly video content review workflow in Notion. Connects to a Notion video database, identifies unprocessed videos (missing category and date stamp), categorizes them, applies tags, and date-stamps them. Use this skill any time the user wants to review, categorize, or tag videos in their Notion database. Trigger on "app content", "review videos", "categorize videos", "video review", "content review", "tag videos", "weekly video review", or any mention of processing new video entries, even if they don't say "video review" explicitly. If the user mentions Amy's video database or weekly content tasks, this is likely the right skill.

The working instructions are in `SKILL.md`.
