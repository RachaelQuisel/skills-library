# proper-grammar

Remove the comma before a coordinating conjunction (and, but, or, so, yet) when the second half has no subject of its own, so the sentence is a compound predicate and not a compound sentence. Use when Rachael types \"/proper-grammar\", says \"remove the comma after the conjunction\", \"not a compound sentence\", \"stop adding commas\", \"no subject in the second clause\", or pastes text or points at a draft and complains about comma placement. Also use when she asks to tidy up text so it doesn't read like an AI wrote it and the draft has this comma pattern.
