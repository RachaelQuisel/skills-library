# Privacy notice

Automation Briefing contains instructions and an icon. It has no bundled backend, telemetry, credential collection, hooks, or executable runtime scripts.

During use, the host assistant may read public release notes, documentation, pricing, and demo pages; configured GitHub reports; and the verified recipient's Slack conversation to check prior delivery. Reads and writes depend on the host's connected tools, account permissions, and the user's authorization. Instructions alone are not a security boundary.

When authorized, the assistant sends the brief to the configured Slack direct message and writes the same brief plus delivery metadata to the configured GitHub archive. These records remain in those services under their access controls and retention policies. The host assistant and research providers may also process prompts, configuration, and retrieved content under their own policies.

Store only necessary report content and delivery metadata. Keep credentials in the host's credential manager. Do not place tokens, client records, private conversation content, or personal recipient settings in this distributable plugin. Remove archived reports or Slack messages through the respective service to delete them; disconnect integrations to revoke further access.

Report issues through the repository's [issue tracker](https://github.com/RachaelQuisel/Skills/issues).
