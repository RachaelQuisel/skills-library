# Research a question or check a draft against retrieved sources, and identify claims the evidence does not support

This skill helps you research a question or check a draft against retrieved sources, and identify claims the evidence does not support.

## What to share

State the question or decision, share the sources you want considered, and identify the intended reader.

## How to start

Select this skill and describe your request. The assistant uses the information and tools available in that conversation.

## Detailed guidance

The [working instructions](SKILL.md) describe the skill's steps, required tools, and limits.
